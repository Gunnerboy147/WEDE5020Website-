1. About Ponelopele Oracle Secondary School

1.1 Institutional Heritage & Public-Private Partnership

Ponelopele Oracle Secondary School is an award-winning public high school located in Kaalfontein, Midrand, in the Gauteng Province of South Africa. The school was officially established in March 2006 and opened its doors to learners in March 2007 through a historic public-private partnership between Oracle Corporation South Africa and the Gauteng Department of Education (GDE).

Constructed with a modern architectural master plan spanning over two hectares, the institution was founded with the clear mission to provide underprivileged township learners in Kaalfontein, Ebony Park, Ivory Park, and surrounding Midrand communities with world-class facilities, advanced science laboratories, cutting-edge computer infrastructure, and superior teaching standards.

1.2 Vision Statement

“To be a beacon of educational excellence and technological leadership in Africa, empowering every learner from diverse socioeconomic backgrounds to develop critical thinking skills, high academic integrity, and personal resilience to become responsible global citizens and innovators.”

1.3 Mission Statement

  • Deliver comprehensive, high-standard curriculum instruction across Mathematics, Sciences, Commercial Subjects, Humanities, and Information Technology.
  • Foster discipline, self-respect, mutual accountability, and ethical citizenship across our entire learner community.
  • Bridge the digital divide by embedding computers, cloud applications, and multimedia technology into daily pedagogical practice.
  • Provide a safe, nurturing, and intellectually stimulating environment supported by nutrition programmes and extracurricular excellence.
  • Work in close collaboration with parents, the School Governing Body (SGB), alumni, and community leaders to ensure sustained academic excellence.

1.4 School Core Values

  1. Excellence (Ponelopele / Vision): Striving for superior quality in every examination, project, and endeavour.
  2. Integrity: Honesty, transparency, and personal responsibility in all actions.
  3. Discipline: Punctuality, neat school uniform adherence, and structured focus in classroom environments.
  4. Innovation: Embracing technology, problem-solving, and scientific curiosity.
  5. Ubuntu (Community & Respect): Compassion, mutual support, inclusivity, and service to society.

3. Core Academic Pillars (The Four Foundational Areas)

The academic success of Ponelopele Oracle Secondary School is built on four core institutional pillars:

Pillar 1: Academic Books & Digital Resource Utilization

Every registered learner at Ponelopele is guaranteed 100% textbook provision across all registered subjects from Grade 8 through Grade 12. Through our partnership with Oracle, textbook learning is supplemented with digital e-learning platforms, digitized exam archives, and daily reading programmes.

  • One textbook per learner per subject policy strictly enforced.
  • Digital past examination question paper archive available in all nine computer laboratories.
  • Compulsory 30-minute daily morning reading session to build literacy and vocabulary.

Pillar 2: Learner Character, Code of Conduct & Daily Discipline

With an enrolment of over 1,600 learners, Ponelopele cultivates a safe, quiet, and highly focused learning environment grounded in mutual respect.

  • Prompt arrival at 07:30 daily with biometric and manual roll call registers.
  • Strict dress code requiring the full red-and-white school blazer, school tie, grey trousers/skirts, and polished black shoes.
  • Active Representative Council of Learners (RCL) giving learners an institutional voice in student affairs.
  • Zero tolerance for substance abuse, bullying, vandalism, or classroom disruption.

Pillar 3: District Trophies, Competitions & Academic Laureates

Ponelopele Oracle has established itself as one of the highest-performing township secondary schools in the Gauteng province, frequently outperforming well-resourced suburban institutions in pure mathematics, physical sciences, and accounting.

  • Annual recipient of the Johannesburg East District Top Performing Public School Award.
  • Multiple provincial gold and silver medals in the Eskom Expo for Young Scientists.
  • National champions in the Association of Black Accountants of South Africa (ABASA) high school accounting olympiad.

Pillar 4: Public-Private Synergy (Oracle Corporation & GDE)

The ongoing partnership between Oracle Corporation South Africa and the Gauteng Department of Education demonstrates how public education can be elevated through corporate responsibility, technological infrastructure, and strategic mentorship.

  • Nine computer laboratories fully equipped and periodically upgraded with modern terminals.
  • Oracle Academy curriculum certification in Java programming and SQL database administration offered to senior learners.
  • Educator digital upskilling workshops conducted every school term.

4.2 Further Education and Training (FET) Band – Grades 10, 11, and 12

In the senior phase, learners take four compulsory core subjects plus three elective subjects grouped into specialized vocational and university-bound streams:

Compulsory Core Subjects:

  1. English First Additional Language (or English Home Language)
  2. Indigenous Language (Sepedi / IsiZulu / Xitsonga Home Language)
  3. Mathematics (Pure Mathematics) OR Mathematical Literacy
  4. Life Orientation

Elective Subject Streams (Choose 3 Electives):

  • Stream A: STEM & Health Sciences: Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Information Technology (IT) or Geography.
  • Stream B: Commercial & Financial Sciences: Accounting, Business Studies, Economics.
  • Stream C: Information & Technical Services: Computer Applications Technology (CAT), Information Technology, Business Studies.
  • Stream D: Social Sciences & Humanities: History, Geography, Tourism or Religious Studies.

6. Admissions & Feeder Zone Criteria

6.1 Gauteng Department of Education Online Admissions Policy

In compliance with Gauteng Department of Education regulations, Grade 8 applications must be submitted through the official GDE Online Admissions portal. In accordance with the South African Schools Act, Ponelopele Oracle is a Section 21 Quintile 1 No-Fee Public School. Parents are not required to pay mandatory tuition fees, ensuring access to quality education regardless of household income.

6.2 Feeder Zone Priority Ranking (In order of admission preference):

  1. Priority Option 1 (Home Address within School Feeder Zone): Applicant resides permanently within the defined school feeder zone (Kaalfontein, Ebony Park, Ivory Park North). Proof of residence is required.
  2. Priority Option 2 (Sibling Attendance): Applicant has a sibling currently enrolled in Grades 8 through 11 at Ponelopele Oracle Secondary School.
  3. Priority Option 3 (Parent Work Address): Applicant's parent or legal guardian is employed within the school feeder zone boundaries.
  4. Priority Option 4 (Radius Extension): Applicant resides within a 30-kilometre radius of the school.
  5. Priority Option 5 (Beyond Radius): Applicant resides more than 30 kilometres from the school (admitted only if capacity permits).

6.3 Compulsory Documentation Checklist

Please ensure clear copies of the following documents are certified and prepared for verification:

  • Certified copy of Learner's Birth Certificate (or valid Passport / Study Permit for foreign nationals).
  • Certified copy of Parent / Legal Guardian's South African National Identity Document or Passport.
  • Official Proof of Residential Address (Municipal water & lights account, valid lease agreement, or letter from ward councillor).
  • Learner's latest official Grade 7 School Report Card (for Grade 8 entrance) or previous grade report card for transfer applications.
  • Certified transfer card from the previous primary or secondary school.
  • Learner's Clinic Road-to-Health Immunisation Card.

8.2 School Governing Body (SGB)

The School Governing Body is constituted under Section 23 of the South African Schools Act, comprising elected parent representatives, educator representatives, non-teaching staff, learner representatives (RCL), and the school principal. The SGB exercises fiduciary responsibility, formulates school policy, oversees campus maintenance, and ensures community accountability.

9. State-of-the-Art Campus Facilities & ICT Labs

Facilities developed in collaboration with Oracle Corporation South Africa:

  • Nine (9) Dedicated Computer Laboratories: Over 350 networked computer workstations connected via high-speed fibre internet, running licensed educational software and development IDEs.
  • Physics & Chemistry Laboratories: Fully equipped science experiment benches with gas fittings, fume cupboards, and safe chemical storage units.
  • Life Sciences & Biology Lab: Microscopes, specimen storage, anatomical models, and interactive smartboards.
  • School Library & Media Centre: Over 15,000 catalogued physical volumes, daily newspapers, quiet study cubicles, and digital e-readers.
  • National School Nutrition Programme (NSNP) Kitchen: Clean kitchen facility preparing hot, nutritious meals every school day for over 1,600 learners.
  • Multipurpose Sports Grounds: Graded grass football / soccer pitch, regulation netball courts, and volleyball facilities.
  • Security Infrastructure: 24-hour perimeter security guarding, biometric gate check-in, and CCTV surveillance.
