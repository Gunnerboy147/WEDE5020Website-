About Ponelopele Secondary School

Ponelopele Secondary School is an award-winning public high school located in Kaalfontein, Midrand, in the Gauteng Province of South Africa. Founded through a landmark educational partnership, our mandate is delivering high-standard STEM education to township learners completely free of compulsory tuition fees.

Led by Principal Mr. Navhatakali Edward Netshianzwane, our institution provides a supportive, rigorous environment where learners develop academic excellence, discipline, and ethical leadership.

Minimum Pass Requirements for Grades 10–12 (NSC)

To pass a school year and receive the National Senior Certificate, a learner takes 7 subjects and must satisfy these minimum marks:

Subject Requirement Minimum % Mark Corresponding Level Pass Condition
Home Language Compulsory 40% minimum Level 3 or higher Must pass at 40%+. Failure results in year failure.
2 other subjects 40% minimum Level 3 or higher Any 2 subjects in curriculum package.
3 other subjects 30% minimum Level 2 or higher Any 3 subjects in curriculum package.
7th subject Can be failed (0% – 29%) Level 1 Allowed to fail 1 non-home language subject.
⚠️

Critical Warning for Parents & Learners

Note: Achieving less than 40% for your Home Language results in an automatic fail for the year. Even if a learner scores 90% in other subjects, failing Home Language prevents promotion to the next grade or obtaining the National Senior Certificate.

🏆

Types of Matric Passes

Depending on how far above the minimum requirements a learner scores, they will earn one of four certification endorsements:

🏆 Bachelor Pass Pass Type #1

Bachelor’s Degree Pass

University Degree Entrance (South African Universities)

Minimum Subject Requirements:

  • ✓Minimum Level 3 (40%) in Home Language.
  • ✓Minimum Level 4 (50%) in 4 other high-credit subjects.
  • ✓Minimum Level 2 (30%) in 2 other subjects.
Career Outcome: Direct entrance into undergraduate degree studies (BSc, BCom, BA, LLB, BEng).
🎓 Diploma Pass Pass Type #2

Diploma Pass

Universities of Technology & Polytechnic Diplomas

Minimum Subject Requirements:

  • ✓Minimum Level 3 (40%) in Home Language.
  • ✓Minimum Level 3 (40%) in 4 other high-credit subjects.
  • ✓Minimum Level 2 (30%) in 2 other subjects.
Career Outcome: Enables entry to National Diploma programs (e.g., NDip IT, NDip Engineering, NDip Nursing).
📜 Higher Certificate Pass Pass Type #3

Higher Certificate Pass

Colleges, TVET & Vocational Certification

Minimum Subject Requirements:

  • ✓Minimum Level 3 (40%) in Home Language.
  • ✓Minimum Level 3 (40%) in 2 other subjects.
  • ✓Minimum Level 2 (30%) in 3 other subjects.
Career Outcome: Higher Certificate study in TVET colleges, technical trades, and vocational training institutes.
📋 NSC Basic Pass Pass Type #4

NSC (Basic Pass)

Standard High School Graduation Certificate

Minimum Subject Requirements:

  • ✓Passes at 40% in 3 subjects (including Home Language).
  • ✓Passes at 30% in 3 other subjects.
  • ✓1 subject can be failed (0% - 29%).
Career Outcome: National Senior Certificate baseline qualification for workplace readiness and entry-level positions.
