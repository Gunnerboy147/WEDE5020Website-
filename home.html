Welcome to Ponelopele Oracle Secondary School

“Nurturing South Africa’s Future Leaders in Mathematics, Science & Digital Technology.”

Ponelopele Oracle Secondary School is an exemplary public high school situated in Kaalfontein, Midrand, Gauteng. Founded in March 2006 through an extraordinary corporate social investment partnership between Oracle Corporation South Africa and the Gauteng Department of Education (GDE), the institution provides high-standard secondary education to over 1,600 township learners completely free of tuition fees.

Welcome to the official portal of Ponelopele Oracle Secondary School. Established in March 2006 through an extraordinary partnership between Oracle Corporation South Africa and the Gauteng Department of Education (GDE), our institution stands as living proof that world-class, technology-driven public education can flourish right here in Kaalfontein, Midrand.

Our learners thrive because of an uncompromising standard of discipline, passionate educators, and universal access to textbooks and digital laboratories. We believe that background does not determine destiny: with daily dedication, intellectual curiosity, and strong moral character, every child can conquer mathematics, science, and the digital economy.

Our Learners: Discipline, Character & Ambition

At Ponelopele Oracle, every learner wears the red-and-white school crest with dignity. Our learners demonstrate that when state-of-the-art technological resources meet daily discipline and committed mentorship, academic excellence is the natural outcome.

Uniform & Schooled Pride Morning Assembly & Community Solidarity

Disciplined High School Learners

Learners maintain immaculate uniform standards, daily punctuality, and mutual respect across Grades 8 through 12. Our learners actively participate in morning reading circles, after-school STEM revision camps, and peer-to-peer tutoring in Pure Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and Accounting.

  • Matric Pass Rate: 95.9% in the latest national examinations.
  • Cohort Focus: Kaalfontein, Ebony Park, Ivory Park, and Midrand.

Morning Assembly & Unity

Over 1,600 learners unite every morning for assembly, singing the South African national anthem and reciting our school pledge. Guided by an active Representative Council of Learners (RCL), learners cultivate governance, debate, public speaking, and community service skills that prepare them for higher education.

  • Student Body Strength: 1,600+ enrolled learners.
  • Leadership: Representative Council of Learners (RCL) and peer educators.

The Four Core Student Pillars

  • 100% Textbook Allocation: Every learner receives full sets of physical CAPS textbooks in addition to cloud study tablets.
  • Oracle Tech Fluency: Hands-on coding and digital computer training across 9 high-speed broadband computer laboratories.
  • Code of Conduct: Strict zero-tolerance policy against bullying, unauthorized absenteeism, or substance abuse.
  • District & Provincial Olympiads: Regular participants and medalists in Gauteng provincial science and mathematics competitions.
